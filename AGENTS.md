## Interpretation contract

Do not collapse these categories:

- **symbolic / ritual language** — authored conceptual grammar;
- **ethical proposition** — normative hypothesis or design commitment;
- **technical specification** — structured proposal for implementation;
- **reference code** — executable translation of that proposal;
- **validated finding** — requires independent empirical support and is not implied merely by appearing in this repository.

The source artifact explicitly preserves falsifiability and uncertainty. Maintain that orientation.

## Modification rules

When repairing code:

- preserve source provenance;
- prefer adding tests before semantic changes;
- do not silently replace source-derived values with later values;
- document divergences from newer TRIA research;
- separate symbolic metadata from measured variables;
- avoid representing inferred intention, consciousness, sentience, resonance, or somatic state as ground truth unless an explicit input or validated measurement supports it;
- preserve user consent, reversibility, non-domination, and meaningful disagreement.

## Relationship to later repositories

The Institute's newer repositories are research descendants and formalizations, not replacements for the existence of this source-derived lineage.

Use later work to annotate, compare, test, or create versioned successors—not to erase the historical implementation.
